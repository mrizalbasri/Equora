# Equora SEO OpenGraph visual asset

`equora-og.webp` is the official 1200 × 630 WebP social preview card for Equora (OpenGraph & Twitter Card summary_large_image).

## Specifications
- Dimensions: 1200 × 630 px (standard 1.91:1 social share aspect ratio)
- Format: WebP (~64 KB)
- Features:
  - Official transparent Equora brand mark & typography
  - High-contrast navy container (`#091829` with ambient radial glows)
  - Core headline: "Beda wilayah. Beda kebutuhan."
  - Summary: Kemendikdasmen open data, MCDA priority simulation, and GIS mapping
  - Badges: 4 core school facilities indicators (Sanitasi Dasar, Air Minum Layak, Internet Pengajaran, Komputer)
  - Architectural school preview card
